# Bed and sleep poses

`bed.png` is an empty right-facing bed. `tuft-sleep.png` is the existing Tuft pose and is retained byte-for-byte. Each other `<look>-sleep.png` is a prepared candidate for the matching standing look: a right-facing, closed-eye pose with a pillow and a small z. These images are separate sprites, not evidence that any character is asleep.

The fifteen new poses were generated from the approved individual standing cast references, with the Tuft sleep pose as a composition reference. The generated drafts were prepared to 114 × 68 pixels with binary transparency and colours selected from the corresponding standing look and Tuft's pillow. Roll, Backpack and Fringe were revised against their standing references. The supervisor reviewed the full candidate sheets for integrated browser review; owner review remains pending. These are approximations, not exact identities. Recoloured Fan bag and shoe regions, and Knit, Raincoat, Bowtie and Roll shoe regions, use the pose-specific masks. Shoe soles and shading are approximate. Backpack's tied hair is simplified; small garment details also differ from the standing looks.

`recipes.json` retains the standing recipe order and target palettes. Its source entries point at pose-specific grayscale masks and exact source RGB mappings; the masks limit recolouring to intended clothing regions. Mask and palette application requires the sleep-frame consumer. The sprite anchor is [55, 62]; the existing bed composition places the pose 25 pixels right and 24 pixels above the bed's top-left corner at native size.
